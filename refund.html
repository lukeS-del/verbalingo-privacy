Verbalingo Refund Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

If the subscription is not right for you, ask within 14 days of a payment and we will refund it in full. This applies to the first payment and to every renewal.

How to ask for a refund

  • Reply to the receipt email from Paddle, or go to paddle.net and look up your order by email address;
  • or write to us at bekuluk7@gmail.com from the address the subscription is under.

Paddle refunds to the original payment method. It usually arrives within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. Pro ends once the refund is made.

After 14 days

The paid period is not refunded, but you can cancel renewal at any time: Pro stays until the end of the period and you will not be charged again. If something went wrong with a payment, such as a double charge or a charge after cancelling, write to us at any time and we will sort it out.
